## About Me

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering student at Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology, building AI systems that sit at the intersection of **information retrieval**, **large language models**, and **research intelligence**.

My flagship project, **Anthology**, is a production-architecture RAG platform that lets users query and reason over scientific literature. Alongside it, I'm building **PaperLens** — a multimodal AI system for understanding figures, tables, and text in research papers.

I care about shipping real products, not just experiments. My work spans FastAPI backends, vector databases, embedding pipelines, Flutter apps, and everything in between.

📍 Mansa, Punjab, India &nbsp;·&nbsp; 🎓 B.E. CSE · TIET · 2023–2027 &nbsp;·&nbsp; 📬 Open to internships

## Featured Projects

### 🔬 Anthology — Research Intelligence Platform
> **Flagship · Active Development**

An advanced RAG platform built to make scientific literature queryable and actionable. Anthology ingests research papers, builds hybrid retrieval indexes over PostgreSQL + pgvector, and answers research questions with citations and structured reasoning.

- **Stack:** Python · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · pgvector · LangChain · Sentence Transformers
- **Architecture:** Hybrid retrieval (dense + sparse) · Re-ranking · Evaluation pipelines
- **Goal:** Production-grade research intelligence — not a demo, a real system

[![Anthology](https://img.shields.io/badge/View%20Repository-Anthology-4A90C4?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Rupinder51120/Anthology)

---

### 🔭 PaperLens — Multimodal Research AI
> **Active Development**

A multimodal AI platform for extracting and reasoning over the full content of scientific documents — including figures, tables, equations, and text — using vision-language models and advanced retrieval.

- **Stack:** Python · Vision-Language Models · Multimodal Transformers · FastAPI
- **Focus:** Document layout understanding · Cross-modal retrieval · Scientific insight extraction
